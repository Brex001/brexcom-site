The Methods

5 Ways to Earn
From Any Device

Each method below can be started for free. Pick one, master it, then layer the others on top.

01

Affiliate Marketing

Share a special link to someone else's product. When someone clicks and buys, you earn a commission — typically 5% to 50%. No product, no stock, no shipping. Just your link.

Best for beginners

02

Display Advertising

Place ads on your website or blog. Ad networks like Google AdSense or Ezoic pay you every time a visitor sees or clicks an ad. The more traffic, the more you earn — automatically.

100% passive

03

Sponsored Content

Once you have an audience, brands pay you to write or post about their products. Rates range from R500 to R50,000+ per post depending on your reach.

High earning potential

04

Digital Products

Sell an e-book, template, checklist, or mini-course once — and get paid hundreds of times. Create it once with tools like Canva or Google Docs and sell via Gumroad or Payhip.

Scales infinitely

05

Email List Monetisation

Build a free newsletter. Send helpful content. Include affiliate links or promote your own products. An email list is an asset you own — no algorithm can take it from you.

Long-term asset

The Roadmap

Your 7-Step
Launch Plan

Follow these steps in order. Each one takes under an hour.

  1. 01

    Pick Your Niche

    Choose one topic you know or enjoy — cooking, fitness, finance, tech, parenting. Narrow is better. "Budget cooking for students" beats "food" every time. Google rewards specificity.

  2. 02

    Set Up Free Hosting

    Launch your site on a free platform. No coding required. Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages, and Netlify all offer free static hosting. For a blog-style site, use Blogger (Google) or WordPress.com free tier.

    Cloudflare Pages GitHub Pages Netlify Blogger
  3. 03

    Get a Free Domain (or Use a Subdomain)

    Use a free subdomain (yoursite.netlify.app) to start. When ready, upgrade to a paid .com domain (~R150/year via Afrihost or Namecheap). A custom domain builds trust and helps SEO.

    Afrihost Namecheap Freenom (free domains)
  4. 04

    Apply for Ad Networks & Affiliate Programs

    Sign up for Google AdSense (display ads) and 1–2 affiliate programs relevant to your niche. Place your affiliate links naturally in your content. You earn whether you're awake or asleep.

    Google AdSense Ezoic Amazon Associates ShareASale
  5. 05

    Link to a Payment Platform

    Connect PayPal, Payoneer, or a local SA option to receive your earnings. Most affiliate networks and ad platforms support these. Payoneer works well for South African creators receiving USD.

    PayPal Payoneer Yoco (SA) Peach Payments (SA)
  6. 06

    Optimise for Google (SEO Basics)

    Write helpful, specific content. Use your niche keywords in headings and the first paragraph. Submit your site to Google Search Console (free). Add a sitemap. Speed = ranking. Fast pages win.

    Google Search Console PageSpeed Insights
  7. 07

    Drive Free Traffic

    Share content on Pinterest (massive traffic driver), Reddit, Facebook Groups, and WhatsApp communities. Post consistently — 2x per week minimum. Traffic = income. No traffic = no income.

    Pinterest Reddit

Stay on Top of Google

SEO Tips That
Actually Work

You don't need to be a tech expert. These basics handle 80% of your ranking.

Use Specific Keywords

Target phrases like "best budget blender under R500" not just "blenders". Specific = less competition = easier to rank.

Fast Load Speed

Google penalises slow sites. Cloudflare Pages is one of the fastest free options globally. Test your speed at pagespeed.web.dev.

Submit to Search Console

Register your site at Google Search Console. Submit your sitemap. This tells Google your site exists and to index it.

Write Helpful Content

Google's algorithm rewards genuinely useful pages. Answer one specific question per page, better than anyone else online.

Use Clear Headings

Structure pages with H1, H2, H3 tags containing your keywords. This helps both humans and search engines understand your content.

Mobile-First Design

Over 60% of searches happen on phones. Your site must look great on small screens. Google ranks mobile performance first.

Get Backlinks

Ask related websites to link to your content. Each link is a "vote" for your site. Comment on forums, answer questions on Quora with your link.

Post Consistently

Fresh content signals an active site. Even one new page per week compounds dramatically over 6–12 months.
