III

Your 8-Step AI Income
Launch Plan

From zero to first payment — follow this in order. Most steps take under 30 minutes.

Step 01

Pick One AI Income Method

Choose the single method above that excites you most. Don't try all 7 at once. Master one, earn from it, then expand. AI copywriting is the fastest to monetise for beginners.

Step 02

Set Up Your Free AI Toolkit

Create free accounts on ChatGPT and Canva at minimum. These two tools alone can generate income in almost every category. Takes 10 minutes total.

ChatGPT Canva Claude

Step 03

Create Your Free Seller Profile

List your AI-assisted service on Fiverr and Upwork (both free). Write a clear, specific gig title: "I will write SEO blog posts using AI — delivered in 24 hours." Specificity = sales.

Fiverr Upwork

Step 04

Build a Portfolio in 1 Day

Use AI to create 3–5 sample pieces before you get any clients. Upload them to a free Notion page or Google Drive folder. "Here are examples of my work" converts browsers into buyers.

Notion (free)

Step 05

Launch Your 1-Page Website

Host a simple page describing your AI services on Netlify or Cloudflare Pages — both completely free. Even a basic page makes you 3x more credible than a Fiverr-only seller.

Netlify Cloudflare Pages

Step 06

Connect Your Payment Method

Set up Payoneer to receive USD from international clients — essential for SA freelancers. PayPal for smaller local payments. Both are free to create and link to your bank.

Payoneer PayPal Gumroad (digital products)

Step 07

Add Affiliate Links for Passive Income

Join affiliate programs for the AI tools you use. Recommend ChatGPT Plus, Canva Pro, ElevenLabs — and earn a commission every time someone signs up through your link. Passive income on top of active income.

Canva Affiliate ElevenLabs Affiliate

Step 08

Drive Traffic With Short-Form Video

Record a 60-second phone video: "I earn money using AI — here's how I start." Post on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts. This is the fastest free traffic source in 2026 — faster than Google by months.
